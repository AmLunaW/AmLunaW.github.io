I am an Assistant Professor in the [Department of Systems and Industrial Engineering](https://sie.engineering.arizona.edu/) at the [University of Arizona](https://www.arizona.edu/). My research develops optimization and data-driven decision-support methods for operational decisions made under uncertainty, when demand is unpredictable, time is critical, resources are limited, and disruptions such as wildfires and extreme weather can change conditions quickly. I apply these methods to problems that touch daily life and carry direct social impact, spanning:

- **Logistics and supply chain management:** placing inventory to meet fast-fulfillment demand, planning delivery routes in time-sensitive environments, designing fleets across shipping options, and enabling cooperative procurement and distribution for small and medium-sized enterprises.
- **Sustainable food and agricultural systems:** allocating and packaging rescued food under uncertain demand, managing perishable inventory and donations, scheduling food redistribution fairly under limited capacity, and optimizing irrigation in arid regions.
- **Resilient and safe transportation infrastructure:** guiding post-wildfire highway maintenance and vegetation recovery, and evaluating the impact of driver-safety programs on crash risk.

Prior to joining the University of Arizona, I received my Ph.D. in Industrial Engineering from the [Wm Michael Barnes '64 Department of Industrial and Systems Engineering](https://engineering.tamu.edu/industrial/index.html) at [Texas A&M University](https://www.tamu.edu/index.html) under the supervision of Prof. [Joseph Geunes](https://engineering.tamu.edu/industrial/profiles/geunes-joe.html) and Prof. [Xiaofeng Nie](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=2cSk8Y4AAAAJ&hl=en). I also hold both an M.S. and a B.S. in Transportation from Southeast University, China.
